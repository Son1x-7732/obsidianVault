# Stretch

---

## Definition

Stretch is flexible empty space inside a layout.

It expands automatically to occupy available space.

---

## Purpose

Stretch is used to align widgets without manually specifying positions.

---

## Example

```cpp
layout->addStretch();

layout->addWidget(label);

layout->addStretch();

```
