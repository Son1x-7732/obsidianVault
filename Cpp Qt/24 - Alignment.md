# Alignment

---

## Definition

Alignment tells a layout how to position a widget inside the space allocated to it.

Layouts decide **where** a widget goes.

Alignment decides **how** it sits inside that space.

---

## Common Alignments

Qt::AlignLeft

Qt::AlignCenter

Qt::AlignRight

Qt::AlignTop

Qt::AlignBottom

These flags can be combined.

Example:

Qt::AlignHCenter | Qt::AlignTop

---

## Key Takeaways

- Layout controls placement.
- Alignment controls positioning within that placement.
- Stretch controls distribution of empty space.
